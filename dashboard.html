What this page counts: real people using the wiom.tv website. Since 31 Jul, the Wiom customer app auto-plays wiom.tv invisibly inside a small promo card on its "not serviceable" screen. Those auto-plays fire the same tracking events as real visits and reached ~10× organic volume — a naive counter would show ~5,000 daily "sessions" when real usage is ~550. Every number on this page excludes that auto-play noise; the Real visits vs app auto-play chart shows what was excluded, and genuine visitors who tapped through from the app are counted there as their own group.
