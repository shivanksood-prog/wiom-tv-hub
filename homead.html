Pre-registration — written before the first user was enrolled

Judged on installs (Home connections), read as a funnel from leading to lagging. Two looks; only the second decides. Nothing below changes after launch.

ItemRule
Unit · armswiom.tv uid, hash keyed on ctId when present, salt homead_v1, 50/50 sticky. HA = ads, HC = control. Orthogonal to fse_v3. Enrolment proof = homead_enrol after ad_config.json loads; a client that cannot reach the config is excluded, never mis-stamped.
CohortEvery uid with homead_enrol on/after the cutoff, intention-to-treat (exposure not required). Primary cut = uids whose device is new since the cutoff; returners reported separately. Both rails enrol; the readout is powered on the phone (sachet) rail only. Prior Home customers (any OTP_VERIFIED or custGen payment before enrolment) excluded from both arms.
Primary day 35 Home connection live (TASKVANILLA_AUDIT OTP_VERIFIED) within 14 d of enrolment, phone rail, HA vs HC. Baseline ~0.4%. This is the KRA; it is not powerable for a subtle lift on pilot traffic, so the scale rule below carries a booking fallback.
Leading funnel daily · day 10 Read in this order, HA vs HC: CTA click per enrolled uid → app installed / launched within 14 d (CleverTap, by MOBILE) → booking created within 14 d → connection live within 14 d. Bookings are the early warning: at the interim, if HA bookings are not above HC, the page says so loudly even though the decision waits.
SecondaryServiceability check · sachet repurchase (guardrail) · P1 vs P0 pre-roll sub-split inside the unserviceable rail. Reported, never decisive.
