WIOM TV — Privacy Policy

Wiom Broadband Private Limited

Last updated: 6 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wiom Broadband Private Limited ("WIOM", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the WIOM TV application ("the App") on your television or streaming device. By using the App you agree to this Policy.

In short: WIOM TV collects the minimum needed to sign you in, keep streams playing smoothly, and understand how the App is used. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

1. Information we collect, and why

a) WiFi network information (precise-location permission)

To sign you in automatically when your television is connected to a Wiom network, the App reads the name (SSID) and identifier (BSSID) of the WiFi network you are connected to. On Android, reading WiFi network information requires the "precise location" permission; the App uses this permission only to read WiFi network information and does not collect GPS coordinates or track your physical location. This is optional — if you decline the permission, or you are not on a Wiom network, the App simply asks you to sign in another way and works normally.

b) Mobile phone number

If you sign in with a mobile number, we send a one-time password (OTP) to that number to verify it. Your phone number is used to identify your household/account. Providing a phone number is optional — you can sign in by scanning a QR code, or continue as a guest without signing in. We do not store your phone number in plain text; for analytics it is used only in a hashed (irreversible) form.

c) Device identifier

The App uses your device's Android ID (a per-device, per-app identifier) as a stable key to recognise your device across sessions for sign-in and analytics. The App does not use the Google Advertising ID.

d) App activity and performance

To understand engagement and keep streams reliable, we collect events describing how the App is used — for example which channels are viewed, channel switching, use of the channel grid, sign-in steps, playback quality (start time, buffering, reconnects), and crash and diagnostic information (including automatic detection of app-not-responding conditions).

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To play and recover streams reliably.
  • To measure engagement and stability, and to improve the App.
  • To communicate with you about the service and, where relevant, to send you relevant messages and offers about WIOM TV.

3. Who we share information with

We use the following service providers, which process data only on our behalf and under contract, not for their own purposes:

  • i2e1 — our WiFi network provider, which enables automatic sign-in when you are on a Wiom network (receives WiFi SSID/BSSID, the device identifier, and, for OTP sign-in, your mobile number).
  • CleverTap — our analytics and engagement provider (receives the device identifier, app-activity events, and a hashed phone identifier).
  • Google Firebase — our crash-reporting and diagnostics provider (receives crash and diagnostic data).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent purposes.

4. Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed for the purposes described above, and as required by applicable law. Aggregated or de-identified data may be retained longer.

5. Security

All information is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it.

6. Your choices

  • You can decline the location permission; automatic sign-in will not run, but the App continues to work.
  • You can sign in by QR code, or continue as a guest without giving a phone number.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time to stop all collection.

7. Data deletion

To request deletion of the data associated with your device or account, email us at legal@wiom.in or privacy@wiom.in and we will action your request in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children

WIOM TV is a general-audience television app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
