Wiom TV — what changed: vc81 → vc82

Everything that differs between the build submitted to the Play Store (vc81 / 13.1.0, rejected on TV quality) and the latest release candidate (vc82 / 13.1.1, committed 1b145f8, signed AAB ready).

vc81 rejected — icon quality vc82 verified on Mi TV AAB: wiom-tv-vc82-13.1.1.aab in Downloads
Why Play rejected vc81: “icon does not fill entire icon space.” The launcher applies a circular mask to every icon — vc81’s rounded-card icon with a pink border got its corners cropped and looked broken. Everything below is what vc82 changes in response, plus the TV app-quality (TV-xx) hardening done on top.

5 · Flagged — decisions & known accepted risks

Guest login wall on every reopen (product decision needed). Guest sessions are deliberately not persisted (#P1). That was invisible when BACK never exited — but vc82’s double-BACK exit makes cold reopens routine, so a guest household (and the Play reviewer) now hits the login screen every single time they reopen the app. Decide: persist guest, or keep the re-login nudge.
Play “missing native symbols” warning will persist. The only native libs are prebuilt AndroidX .so files that ship already stripped — there are no symbols to extract. Cosmetic warning, not a review blocker.
Content licensing posture unchanged (standing risk). 257 of 258 channels remain rights-unverified (founder decision: ship all, kill-switch as mitigation). This was the top rejection/takedown risk pre-vc81 and is unaffected by vc82.
