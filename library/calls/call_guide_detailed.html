Caller Sheet — "What's working well?"

Find the job Wiom TV is hired to do — the underlying need it fulfils and what users genuinely value — to identify the single biggest needle-moving JTBD. Start from what's working / loved, not what's broken.

Built on the Switch interview (Moesta/Spiek), the Four Forces & The Mom Test (Fitzpatrick).  |  Target: pre-WiFi / first-time-internet Bharat household · Android TV · Hindi-speaking · often watched by elders.

Golden rules — read before every call

  1. Ask about the past and the specific, never the hypothetical. "Tell me about the last time you watched" beats "Would you watch more if…". Real events are data; future opinions are noise.
  2. Don't pitch, don't lead, don't sell. The moment you explain or defend a feature, the data is contaminated. Be a curious journalist, not a salesperson.
  3. Shut up and let silence work. After they answer, wait. The second sentence is usually the honest one.
  4. Dig on emotion and effort. On feeling ("achha laga", "bachche khush") or work done (told a friend, kept it after wifi removed), STOP and ask "why" 2–3 times.
  5. Anchor every claim to a moment. "I watch daily" → "When did you last watch? What time? Who was in the room? What did you put on first?"
  6. Record names of channels/shows verbatim. Exact titles are the strongest signal of the job.

Warm-up

build trust · get context
Tell me a bit about your home — who all watch TV, and what do they usually watch?
अपने घर के बारे में थोड़ा बताइए — TV कौन-कौन देखता है, और आमतौर पर क्या देखते हैं?
Before Wiom TV, how did everyone in the house watch TV / shows?
Wiom TV से पहले घर में सब TV या शो कैसे देखते थे? (cable, mobile, YouTube?)
Listener note: you're mapping the "old way" — the thing Wiom TV replaced. That's half the job.
1

The Switch

reconstruct the moment of adoption
Take me back to when you first got Wiom TV. What made you try it? What was going on at home around that time?
याद कीजिए जब आपने पहली बार Wiom TV लिया था। आपने try क्यों किया? उस समय घर में क्या चल रहा था?
What were you hoping it would do for you / your family?
आपको लगा था कि ये आपके या परिवार के लिए क्या करेगा?
Walk me through the very first time you used it — who was there, what did you put on?
सबसे पहली बार जब चलाया — कौन था साथ में, सबसे पहले क्या लगाया?
Push & Pull What was frustrating about the old way (push)? What pulled them to Wiom TV (pull)?
2

The job today

"what's working well?" — the heart of the call
Tell me about the last time you used Wiom TV — when was it, what time of day, who was in the room, what did you watch first?
पिछली बार जब Wiom TV चलाया — कब था, किस समय, कमरे में कौन था, सबसे पहले क्या देखा?
On a normal day, when does Wiom TV get switched on, and what for?
आम दिन में Wiom TV कब चालू होता है, और किसलिए?
What do you / your family enjoy most about it? (let them name channels/shows — write them down exactly)
आपको या परिवार को इसमें सबसे ज़्यादा क्या अच्छा लगता है? (कौन से channel / show — पूछिए नाम)
What does Wiom TV let you do that you couldn't do before?
Wiom TV से आप क्या कर पाते हैं जो पहले नहीं कर पाते थे?
Listener note: the need hides in WHO + WHEN + WHAT-FIRST + the feeling word. Push "why is that good for you?" until you hit a human outcome (peace, bachche busy, saved money, feels like cable, time-pass for elders).
3

The "miss it" test

substitution → proves importance
If Wiom TV stopped working tomorrow, what would you do instead?
अगर कल Wiom TV बंद हो जाए, तो उसकी जगह आप क्या करेंगे?
How would that feel? What would you miss the most?
कैसा लगेगा? सबसे ज़्यादा किस चीज़ की कमी खलेगी?
Has there been a day it didn't work — what did the family do? What did they say?
कोई दिन ऐसा हुआ जब नहीं चला — तब घरवालों ने क्या किया, क्या कहा?
Listener note: strong emotion here = a real job; a shrug = a weak job. The thing they'd switch back to reveals the true competitor.
4

Effort & advocacy

have they "hired" it for real?
Have you told anyone else about Wiom TV? Who, and what did you tell them?
आपने किसी और को Wiom TV के बारे में बताया? किसको, और क्या बताया?
Did you change anything at home because of it — removed cable, moved the TV, bought something?
इसकी वजह से घर में कुछ बदला — cable हटवाया, TV जगह बदली, कुछ खरीदा?
Habit & Anxiety What's become a habit (= retention)? What still makes them nervous — will it stay free? will channels go? (= the brake to remove).
5

Close

open-ended · catch the unexpected
If you could wave a magic wand and add one thing to Wiom TV, what would it be? (note it — don't promise anything)
अगर एक चीज़ Wiom TV में जोड़ सकते, तो क्या जोड़ते?
Anything I didn't ask about that you want to tell me?
कुछ और जो मैंने नहीं पूछा पर आप बताना चाहें?

After the call — capture immediately

3 minutes, while it's fresh. Log each call into the capture template.
  • Job statement: "When ___ (situation), I want to ___ (motivation), so I can ___ (outcome)."
  • Old way it replaced + what they'd switch back to (the real competitor)
  • Channels / shows named, verbatim
  • Strongest emotion word they used (in their language)
  • 2–3 raw verbatim quotes (don't paraphrase)
  • Push / Pull / Habit / Anxiety — one line each
  • Who watches + when + what-first
Keep buckets emergent — do NOT pre-assign categories. Themes get mined across all calls later.
