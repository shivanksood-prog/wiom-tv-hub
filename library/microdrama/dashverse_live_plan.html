The Dashverse promos, playing live on Wiom TV

We already hold links to 20 Dashverse promos — over 3 hours of watchable microdrama. Here is how they become a channel on wiom.tv where a promo plays, and a payment popup appears when it ends. Nothing needed from Dashverse to start.

₹0 / month to run Live in ~2 weeks Nothing needed from Dashverse

What we have today

A catalog of 20 shows — promos and posters, shared as Google Drive links, from 1 to 33 minutes each (~3.3 hours of content — the longer ones are effectively full pilot episodes). We verified the links download and play correctly. They carry a DashReels watermark; we go live with it as-is, and swap in clean full episodes whenever Dashverse sends them — without redoing any of this work.

How the promos go live

Four steps, all in our hands.

We copy the 20 promos from the shared links

A one-time pull from the Google Drive links Dashverse already shared — already tested and working. Drive links can't be streamed to viewers directly, so the files move to a proper home once.

Wiom

We prepare them once

Each promo is compressed to about 9× smaller — so it streams smoothly on any phone and is light on viewers' data. All 20 shows take about two hours, on a laptop.

Wiom

Promos go on a free delivery network

The files sit on Cloudflare — a global network that delivers video to viewers directly. Wiom's own server never carries the video, which is why the monthly running cost is ₹0, even if viewership grows 10×.

Wiom

A new channel appears on wiom.tv

Flip to the channel and a promo starts playing from the beginning. When it ends, a payment popup appears — the moment a hooked viewer can pay to unlock more. Every play and every tap on that popup is measured from day one, so we learn what converts before spending anything on full shows.

What we need to start

Internal

One company Cloudflare account

Free to use; needs a company card on file to activate and a named owner. Ten-minute setup once decided.

Internal

Approve a small web address

A dedicated address for the video files (about ₹1,000/year). Keeps the launch date fully in our control — no changes to wiom.tv's existing setup needed.

Decide

What the payment popup offers

The popup ships on day one and measures every tap. Connecting a live payment gateway — which provider, what's being sold, at what price — is a decision we can take after seeing the first week of tap data.

Timeline

From day 1

Numbers flow in — which promos get watched to the end, and who taps the payment popup.

What comes later, without redoing anything: when Dashverse sends clean full episodes, they drop into the same pipes — same delivery network, same channel — and the watermarked promos are swapped out. The payment popup then unlocks real full shows instead of capturing interest.
