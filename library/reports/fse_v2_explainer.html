Wiom TV · Experiment brief · fse_v2

Where should a first session land? Four genres go head-to-head.

Every new wiom.tv visitor boots into the same hardcoded channel. fse_v2 randomizes that one decision — the landing genre — across four equal cohorts, on top of a health-gated channel ranking that ships to everyone. Nothing else differs between arms. One pre-registered metric decides it.

Mechanics

One hash, four sticky cohorts, one changed variable

New users only. Existing users keep their current boot channel and are excluded from analysis (they do get the new ranking — it ships globally). Assignment is client-side, deterministic, and auditable: the hub recomputes every user's hash and flags mismatches.

new uid arriveshash(uid + '|fse_v2') % 4 → sticky forever
LE 25%lands on Entertainment

Each arm boots into its genre's hero — the top channel that is verifiably healthy (≥50% start-coverage, ≤5% errors/tune, enough data to be confident) and popular by Wilson-bounded stick-rate. The genre is what's randomized; the hero channel floats with the data. If a genre has no healthy channel at boot, the session falls back to Entertainment and logs itself (guardrail: <5%). After the first channel, the four arms are pixel-identical.

The health-gated ranking (spec 5) is the floor this runs on

fse_v1's ranking promoted broken channels to top slots (a channel with 50% instant skip-away held rank 3 for four days). Before fse_v2 starts, the ranking gets three fixes — so every arm's landing hero and guide order are trustworthy.

FixRuleEffect (simulated on live data)
Health gateA channel is condemned only on confident evidence: ≥20 tunes AND Wilson-95 bounds prove coverage <50% or errors >5%/tune. Thin channels (4 tunes, 1 error) stay "unknown" — explored mid-pack, never headlined.Broken channels pin to genre bottom. Shemaroo Josh & Hi Dost! leave Entertainment's top 2; Dangal TV takes rank 1.
Wilson rankingRank by the 95% lower bound of stick-rate, needing ≥30 tunes and ≥8 distinct tuners.7-of-39-users flukes stop outranking 51-of-347 facts; daily rank-1 churn stops.
Dead-channel purgeFive channels with zero successful starts on real volume removed from the catalog.~15–20% of today's dead-channel skips disappear for every arm.
Measurement — locked at deploy, no metric shopping

One metric decides. Everything else supports or protects.

MetricRoleWhy this role
First-session activation≥1 channel held ≥60s in the first session Primary Landing is a first-session lever — this is the metric it must move. Three comparisons (LN/LM/LU vs LE) at Bonferroni-corrected α=0.0167 each.
D1 retention · installs · watch-min · unmute Secondary Support the story, never decide it. A D1 win without an activation win spawns a follow-up experiment — it does not switch the landing.
Stream-error hit rate Guardrail No arm may route users into more broken streams. The health gate should make landings safer — this verifies it.
Sample ratio (1:1:1:1) · hash audit · landing-fallback <5% · hero stability Guardrail Integrity first: a lift on invalid data is worthless. All checked hourly on the hub before any effect is read.
Timeline & power

Two looks. No peeking between them.

~312 new users/day → ~78 per arm per day. fse_v1's lesson is baked in: its day-2 "+7.9pp winner" at p=0.073 evaporated within 48 hours. Verdicts happen at pre-committed dates only.

Interim — no decision

Integrity, guardrails, and direction only. Pairwise sensitivity is still ≈5pp uncorrected — any "significant winner" this early is a peeking artifact by construction.

Final — decision

Detects ≈4.5pp (Bonferroni-corrected, 80% power). A challenger that clears it becomes the default landing. Nothing clears it → Entertainment stays.

Decision rules — agreed before data exists

Every outcome already has an answer

What the data says at day 12What we do
A challenger beats Entertainment at corrected p < 0.0167Its genre becomes the default landing for everyone.
Two challengers clear the barHighest point estimate wins; runner-up seeds the next test.
Nothing clears the barEntertainment stays. A true effect smaller than ~4.5pp isn't worth a default switch — status-quo bias is correct under a null.
A secondary (say D1) wins but activation doesn'tNo switch. It becomes the pre-registered primary of a follow-up — signals get their own experiment, not a retroactive promotion.
A guardrail breaks (SRM, errors, fallback>5%)Effects are unreadable — fix the pipeline first. Integrity precedes interpretation.
Recorded trade-off: there is no un-sorted control arm. The health-gated sort ships to 100% on judgment — it's free, provably harmless, and was directionally positive — not on proof. fse_v1 ruled out sort effects ≥5pp on activation, and by Jul 11 every residual "commitment" signal had faded too (D1 down to +1.1pp, p=0.59). The cost of this choice: the sort's causal effect on retention is now permanently unmeasurable. If that question ever matters again, it needs a fresh 5–10% holdback — rejected today to keep all four landing arms at full power.
Three things that need a conscious yes before hand-off to Satyam:
  • LM boots into Bhojpuri Cinema. That is the Movies catalog after honest health-gating. Approving the arm approves the language skew.
  • The ~4.5pp decision bar. Smaller true effects deliberately resolve to "keep Entertainment". A default switch should demand a real effect.
  • Sequencing: gesture-unmute and the Hindi toggle deploy at look boundaries only — both change first-session behavior and would contaminate the primary mid-window.
How we got here

fse_v2 is built from fse_v1's scar tissue

Jul 3–7 · the incident

Deployed but never reloaded

fse_v1 ran 4 days with variant data silently dropped. Hence: reload in the checklist, variant rows verified within the hour.

Jul 8–10 · the whiplash

+7.9pp → +0.5pp

An early "winner" (p=0.073) collapsed as clean cohorts accrued — winner's curse in the wild. Hence: two pre-committed looks, no verdicts between.

Jul 10 · the autopsy

The ranking promoted junk

Health analysis found broken channels in top slots and five dead channels in the catalog. Hence: spec 5's gate, and error rate as a standing guardrail.
