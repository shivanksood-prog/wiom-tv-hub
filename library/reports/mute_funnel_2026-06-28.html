9 in 10 sessions watch silent TV. Of 317 sessions explicitly shown the “tap to unmute” prompt, only 36 (11.4%) turned sound on — and 21 of those 36 muted again. Sound is the core of the medium; right now the product is being consumed as a silent video wall.

Mobile autoplay policy is the wall. Mobile (97% of traffic) unmutes at half the desktop rate. The prompt is being seen but not acted on — worth testing a louder affordance, sound-on-first-tap-anywhere, or a persistent unmute banner until audio is enabled.

3 · Channel Changes per Session + Duration

Sessions bucketed by how many times the channel changed (tunes), with how long they lasted and how much was watched. Span = time from first to last event; watch = summed per-channel dwell.

Channels changedSessionsShareMedian spanAvg spanMedian watchAvg watch
Engagement is monotonic in surfing — but the bottom is hollow. 311 of 641 sessions (49%) changed the channel 0–1 times and watched essentially nothing (median <10s). The top bucket (21+ changes, 50 sessions) is the real audience: ~5.4 min median session, ~4.3 min watched. The job is to move the 0–1 bucket up the ladder — better default landing channel + faster playback.
