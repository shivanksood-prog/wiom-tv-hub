The CJP protest turned wiom.tv into a news screen.

The protest ran the experiment for us: two hard-dated demand shocks, fourteen days into a stable data era, with no product change in between. Reach barely moved. Depth moved a lot — and every depth metric points the same way.

The Cockroach Janta Party — a youth movement formed after the Chief Justice likened India's young to "cockroaches" — spent July demanding the Education Minister's resignation over the exam paper-leak scandal. It marched on 20 July and won on 25 July.

Who did the watching

We became the news screen for people who already had us — not a news destination.

The need was real and we served it. But no new demand walked in the door: through the biggest news week of the year, new-user arrivals were flat. Everything moved inside the base we already had.

74%of the extra hours came from repeat users — who are only 27% of DAU

Repeat users are just over a quarter of daily actives and delivered three quarters of the increment (+8.5 of the +11.4 h/day), with their news hours up +328%. New users did go deeper too — hours +30%, news hours +128% — there were simply no more of them. Nobody discovered wiom.tv because of the protest.

And where we did not serve the need: users who sampled 2–5 minutes of news return at 9.2% D1 against 12.3% for the rest of the catalogue. Some people arrived at news and bounced straight off it.

Reach flat, depth up, and the depth is news-shaped

DAU moved +6% while watch hours moved +73% — so this was not acquisition. It survives whale-stripping, so it was not one heavy household. What grew was how far the existing base went: P90 watch +58%, P95 +87%, 15-minute users as a share of DAU +78%. And news took 73% of every incremental hour while going from a quarter of watch time to nearly a half. The demand pre-dated the protest — news was already 24% of watch time at baseline. The week revealed the ceiling of that demand; it did not create it.
