Old vs New Wiom TV App

First read after Play rollout · data 15 Jun – 27 Jul 2026 · CleverTap (old = WRZ, new = WW8) · built 28 Jul
One week into rollout, the new app already carries ~26% of all Wiom TV watch-hours — and users who switched are watching more, not less.
  • Switched users (n=43): 11.2 → 17.1 min/day pooled (+53%), median 1.8 → 4.3 min/day. 27 up >20%, 12 down, only 2 went dark.
  • Fresh installs, same weekdays: more reach content on day 0 (72% vs 60%) and watch longer (median 6.2 vs 4.5 min; mean 28 vs 8.9 min).
  • Watch it: the two heaviest old-app watchers dropped hard after switching (92→22 and 68→25 min/day) — likely a content/EPG gap worth a call.
  • Instrumentation bug found: the new app resets CleverTap identity on every boot, so day-1 return of logged-in fresh users is currently invisible (details below).

Old-app installs ran ~5–12/day through mid-July, then collapsed to ~1/day from 24 Jul as the store started serving the new build. Combined install rate last week (~5–6/day) is below the old norm — worth checking Play rollout %, and store-listing conversion of the new listing assets.

Fix these to trust the next read

  1. Boot identity reset (real bug): WiomTvApp.kt:26 calls onUserLogin("tv_<device>") on every app start, so a logged-in user's next-day session lands back on the device profile, split from their ph_*/wifi_* login profile. Until fixed, retention of logged-in fresh users is invisible and every funnel joining across login undercounts. Fix: restore the saved auth identity at startup instead of re-asserting tv_*.
  2. Don't compare tune-in counts across apps: new-app channel_view fires per view-segment (6,464 events vs old 3,558 same week) — it reads 2× busier without being 2× the engagement.
  3. Call the two lapsed heavy users: before/after 92→22 and 68→25 min/day. If the new lineup is missing their channels, that's cheap to learn now.
  4. Migration match is a floor: 43 of 78 new-app units matched to the old app. Lookback only reaches 1 Jun; 11 unmatched ph_* phone-hashes may still be older returning users, so "fresh" is slightly overstated.

Method

Sources: CleverTap event export — old app (Ashutosh's com.wiomtv.android.prod ≤13.0.5) account WRZ-989-957Z; new app (13.1.1, zero-based rebuild) account WW8-46Z-Z87Z. Watch time: old = dwell between consecutive play_first_framed within a session (1s–2h cap, last-in-session excluded); new = Σ channel_view.durationSec (2h cap). Identity: new-app units merged across tv_<device> and ph_<sha256(phone)[:16]> using phone hashes recovered from old-account profiles (13/24 matched); wifi_* (MAC-hash) units can't be linked and stand alone. Migration = device-id or phone-hash present in old account since 1 Jun. All week windows are Tue–Mon; migrated before-windows weekday-match each user's after-window across the 4 prior weeks. Data: ~/wiom_tv/clevertap/data/oldnew/ · scripts oldnew_pull*.py, oldnew_analyse2.py.
