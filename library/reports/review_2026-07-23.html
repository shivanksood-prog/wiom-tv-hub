State of wiom.tv

Watch, activation, the July 20 spike & the channels that carry engagement
data through 23 Jul 2026, 12:22 IST
How every number here is built. Watch = tune-dwell only (sum of tune.duration_sec), capped at 4h per tune to kill left-open-tab outliers (30 such tunes trimmed, −166h); the buggy playback_stall millisecond field is never counted. Unit of analysis is the user (uid), never the session. Days are IST (UTC + 5:30). Only the uid era (from 23 Jun) is used. Genre = the channel's own channel_cat (a real field — no bucketing needed). Activation = a user watching ≥60s total in a day across all channels, over all users who opened the app that day. Era notes: dwell was fixed 27 Jun; a fresh experiment (fse_v2) + 5 dead-channel removals landed 13 Jul — so pre-13-Jul error/unmute rates aren't comparable, but watch-hours are.

What is actually happening: wiom.tv is a steady ~20h/day live-TV habit for a small, slowly-compounding base. News is the engine, Devotional is the deepest-loyalty niche, and the returning-user share is climbing — the healthiest signal in the deck. The wall is unchanged: three of four users who land never reach a minute, so the whole game is the first 60 seconds.
How the live dashboard's number differs. The wiom.tv/dashboard tile "Engaged · watched ≥60s" comes from /api/stats in server.js — rendered as funnel.engaged ÷ funnel.opens — and it is not defined the same way as the ~27% activation above. Both count distinct users (uid) and both read the clean tune.duration_sec field (so neither carries the playback_stall ms bug), but the bar and the window differ:
Live dashboard · "Engaged ≥60s"
  • ≥60s in a single tune — any one channel-dwell crosses 60s
  • Window: rolling 24h (UTC), no day boundary
  • Denominator: users who opened (session_start / app_open)
  • No 4h/tune cap
This review · "Activation"
  • ≥60s total across all channels that day
  • Window: IST calendar day
  • Denominator: everyone with any event that day
  • 4h/tune cap applied
Net: the dashboard is stricter on concentration (needs one continuous 60-second sitting) but looser on window & denominator. A user watching 30s each on two channels is activated here but not "engaged" there; a rolling-24h read can also straddle two IST days. So don't quote the live dashboard's % as the same figure — they will differ.
02

What happened on July 20

July 20 was the biggest watch day of the week — 26.6 hours (+56% over the ~17h daily baseline) and the highest channel-switching of any day (3,751 tunes). The cause is unmistakable in the data: a news day.

Likely cause: a live news event pulled users onto Hindi & regional news channels on 20 Jul — the fingerprint is broad breadth (NDTV 24×7 +27 users, NDTV Rajasthan +25, DD News +30, Republic Bharat +19) plus regional Punjabi/Bhojpuri music, layered on one very long Vedic devotional session. It's a genuine demand spike, not an instrumentation or product change — no deploy landed on the 20th, and the surge is spread across dozens of news channels, exactly what a news day looks like. We can't name the event from telemetry alone.

Read as directional, not like-for-like. The three denominators differ: Meta = share of paid impressions · YouTube = share of self-selected organic views (a warm, already-engaged base — different from paid) · App = share of user-days reaching 60s. These aren't identical bases, so treat them as benchmarks for "how good is our depth," not a common scoreboard. Because Meta ad videos are sub-60s, ad completion is the fairest Meta analog to our 60-second bar. YouTube figures exclude the 63M-view "Ek kahani" viral outlier.
