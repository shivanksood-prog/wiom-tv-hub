Footnote 3 — cx-app promo-card traffic excluded (re-based 13 Aug). From 31 Jul the Wiom customer app auto-played wiom.tv inside an invisible WebView promo card on its Unserviceable screen; every card impression fired full session/tune events at ~10× organic volume. Those are impressions, not visits — no human is watching — so every metric on this page is web-classed traffic only (classification: lib_traffic_class.js, rules in DEFINITIONS.md). App-origin uids are likewise excluded from new-uid counts and every retention/A2HS cohort. Weeks touching 29 Jul onward therefore read far lower than pre-13-Aug snapshots of this page — that is the correction, not a traffic collapse; nothing real exploded on 31 Jul either.

How the chips are decided: rates backed by counts (retention, A2HS, error rates) use a two-proportion z-test; per-day counts and ratios (DAU, watch hours, sessions/user, watch-min/session) use Welch's t across the daily series, because day-to-day spread is much wider than a pooled proportion test would admit; percentiles are compared by bootstrap 95% CI overlap and never given a σ. |z| ≥ 3 = SIGNAL, 2–3 = borderline, < 2 = noise.
