Time to first frame

Daily median and p90 of playback_start.ttff_ms, human sessions. Expect the median to fall first on 3g/4g-class sessions; the p90 is where cascades and heavy streams live.

New vs returning devices

Returning devices were stuck on the 3 July build until sw.js changes. The gap closing is PR #12 landing; both moving down is PR #13.

Errors per 100 loads · holds · retries

After PR #13, errors are not comparable on their own — a hold replaces 5–30 hops and timeout_frame is a new code. Read the three together.

Returning devices on the current build

Share of returning-device sessions (with playback) stamped by the current build. Pre-deploy ~38%; PR #12 should take it to ~90% within a day or two of the sw.js copy. fast-zap = playback_start.meta present.

Warm zaps

Share of starts served from the pre-buffer and their TTFF vs cold starts on the same link class (meta.bw ≥ 4 Mbps). Warm only exists on fast links — never compare against all cold starts.

Pre-buffer data cost

session_end.meta.pf_kb: sessions that pre-buffered, p90 KB per session, and KB on 2g/3g sessions (must stay ~0 — that is the gate working).
